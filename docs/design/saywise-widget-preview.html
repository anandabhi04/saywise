Floating widget: vertical, with Pause and Stop

The widget sits on the right edge of the screen, standing up. While you talk it shows two buttons: Pause and Stop. Pause keeps what you said so far. Press it again to go on from there. You can drag it to the left edge too.

Option A · small pill, middle of the edge

Takes little space. Stays in the middle of the side. Grows only while you talk.

Option B · tall strip, full height of the edge

Runs along the whole side of the screen. The wave fills the strip. Easier to hit, but always takes the full height.
