Why it looks like this

One bar, not a button and a pill

What you click is where the wave appears. One thing to learn, one place to look.

Black and white, red when live

No brand color. The system accent on controls, system red when the mic is open. It reads as part of macOS.

Three parts in the window

Status, button, text. Recent dictations moved to History. Settings moved to the toolbar.

Two words of status

"Ready", "Listening", "Finishing", "Mic is off". The hint after the dot says the one thing you can do next.

One step per page

Setup asks for one thing at a time, in the order it is needed. Done steps are skipped.

Never steals focus

The bar is a non-activating panel. Clicking it leaves the cursor in the app you are typing in.
